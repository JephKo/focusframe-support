FocusFrame Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-11

Overview

FocusFrame helps users manage tasks, priorities, reminders, and journal reflections. We aim to keep your data private and under your control.

Data Stored

FocusFrame may store To-dos, Tasks, Journal entries, preferences, reminders, and related app settings.

Local and iCloud Storage

Data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud/CloudKit sync is enabled, your app data may sync through your Apple iCloud account.

Weather and Calendar Features

If enabled, FocusFrame may request access to WeatherKit, Calendar, or related Apple services to support app features. Permission is requested through Apple’s standard system prompts.

Data Sharing

FocusFrame does not sell your personal data.
