Common Questions

How do I create a To-do?

Tap the plus button in the app, choose To-do, then add a title, priority, date, time, or reminder.

How do I create Tasks?

Tasks can be standalone items or linked to a To-do as subtasks.

How does Journal work?

Use the quick prompts to record a trigger, feeling, intended action, and any extra thoughts.

Does FocusFrame use iCloud?

FocusFrame may use iCloud/CloudKit if enabled, so your data can sync across your Apple devices.

Privacy

FocusFrame does not sell user data. Data is stored locally on your device and may sync through iCloud if enabled.
